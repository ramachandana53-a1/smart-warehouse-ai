# 📦 Smart Warehouse & Autonomous Order Fulfillment Platform

An AI-powered warehouse control tower designed to resolve inventory stockouts, automate order priority calculations, and eliminate costly fulfillment delays in real time.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Traditional Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) are reactive—they report inventory numbers but rely on manual human intervention when emergencies occur. When high-priority or VIP orders face stock shortages, delays lead to missed delivery SLAs, broken contracts, and expensive penalty fees.

**Smart Warehouse** introduces an **Autonomous AI Decision Engine** that continuously monitors live operational telemetry. When stock conflicts or bottlenecks arise, the AI automatically evaluates order priorities and recommends or executes optimal stock reallocations.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **⚡ Autonomous Decision Engine:** Detects critical inventory exceptions in real time and generates multi-strategy resolution paths (e.g., stock reallocation, split shipments, express restocks).
* **🤖 Operations AI Copilot:** A built-in virtual assistant (`Warehouse Helper AI`) allowing supervisors and floor staff to query priority calculations, stock locations, and system status using plain English.
* **🎮 Surprise & Emergency Simulator:** Interactive controls to test system resilience against real-world disruptions like sudden VIP order surges or equipment breakdowns.
* **📊 ROI & Exception Analytics:** Live tracking of operational time saved and late-delivery penalty fees prevented ($1,850+ saved per operational shift).
* **🎯 Pitch & Demo Mode:** Built-in guided walkthroughs designed for quick, zero-latency live demonstrations.
